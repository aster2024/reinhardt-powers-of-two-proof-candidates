# Authorship and AI-use disclosure

The human authors are **Jizhou Guo** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-0699-9164)) and **Yitao Luo** ([email](mailto:luoyt.kd@mail.ustc.edu.cn)); Jizhou Guo initiated and maintains the public repository. Generative-AI tools provided substantial assistance during proof exploration, implementation of searches and verifiers, computational cross-checking, and manuscript preparation. The human authors directed the research, assessed the resulting materials, and take responsibility for the claims, arguments, citations, code, and released artifacts. AI-assisted checks are not treated as independent peer review, so the results are presented as **computer-assisted proof candidates** for direct scrutiny rather than as literature-established theorems.
